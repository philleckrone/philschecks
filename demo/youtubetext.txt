Phils Custom (cms) Checks is a Paratext tool that brings Phil's custom checks to the Tools menu: 19 checks for titles, section headings, quotation marks, footnotes, figures, marker spaces, capitals and more, in English, Spanish and French. Nothing has to be copied into Paratext's cms folder. You choose a check and click Run, and Paratext's own check window opens. Set the check's options there before you run it: they decide what counts as a problem in your language, and Paratext keeps them with the project. A report check lists its findings in Paratext's results list, and many also open an HTML report with the context of each finding. This video runs Report of Capital at Verse on a sample project.
